## What changed
A first-visit tour now teaches operations by rebuilding Mosaic instead of pointing at one over the finished picture.
- **14 steps on a first visit** (was 9). Step 5, Operations, empties the preview. Steps 6–9 open operation 1's card at Selection, Pattern, Attribute and Blend, and the picture shows the All marking, then the tiles. Steps 10 and 11 add operations 2 and 3, and the picture is whole again. **A replay keeps 8 steps**, with one short Operations step.
- **Skip operations** on steps 5–11 goes to Breakpoints. Back from Breakpoints goes to Operation 3.
- Only the preview is staged: the file, undo and the export never see it, and the tour leaves no draft. A first visit whose operations are not Mosaic's three runs as a replay.
- The text is in `apps/editor/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` § "Onboarding: the tour", "Building Mosaic". The editor's own names are bold (**All**, **Noise**, **Tile**, **Set**, **Special**).
