# B-016 Templates and the tile gallery
Backlog: B-016 · Tier: big · Areas: editor-shell, tiles-ui · Decisions: S0, S1, S2 and S3a answers in docs/plans/starters-onboarding.md
Output: unchanged

## What changed
S3a (content and loader, no UI yet): the editor ships its first template, Collage, under `apps/editor/public/templates/collage/`. It was migrated to schemaVersion 4 by `migrate()`, and its embedded photos were re-encoded as WebP q90 (10.8 MB → 0.86 MB, approved by Pol by eye). A manifest, `public/starters.json`, lists the template and the 21 gallery pictures, with committed thumbnails. New `starters.ts` parses the manifest and loads a template or gallery pictures on demand. ImportPanel's open sequence is split into a pure `prepareArchive` (`import.ts`) and `adopt` (`adopt.ts`), so templates will open exactly as an import does.
