## Overview

A list of rows, exactly one chosen, for options that need more than a word: Export image's sizes,
each a label and a width ("Breakpoint 2", "768 px"). A [segmented control](/editor/ui-toggle/)
holds a word per option and no more.

## In detail

### Public surface

Generic over `K extends string`.

| Prop | Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `legend` | `string` | The fieldset's legend, in `.t-label`: the group's name. |
| `name` | `string` | The radios' shared `name`; unique on the page. |
| `options` | `{ key: K; label: string; detail: string }[]` | In display order. `detail` is the mono value at the right. |
| `value` | `K` | Bindable: the chosen option's `key`. |

Options are told apart by `key`, not by the value they stand for: two sizes can share a width (a
breakpoint bound equal to the design width keeps its own row), and a radio group needs one value
per row.

### Behaviour

- The radios are native `<input type="radio">`s, only restyled (`appearance: none`). The legend
  names the group, Tab enters on the chosen row, and the arrow keys move the choice, with no key
  handling in the component.
- The focus ring is drawn on the whole row (`:has(:focus-visible)`), not on the 12px dot.

### Look

- A field-grey well (`--color-field`, `--radius-sm`) of `--control-h` (30px) rows: a 12px ring
  in the text colour, the label, and the `detail` in `.t-console` at the right.
- The chosen row is ink with white text and a filled white dot, so the choice shows by the dot's
  shape as well as by colour (1.4.1). Other rows wash darker under the pointer.
