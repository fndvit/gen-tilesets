## Overview

`TabBar` is the row of four step names under each operation's summary: Selection, Attribute,
Source and Blend. When the operation is closed, all four are black. When it is open, the current
step turns white and joins the panel below it. Clicking a step opens the operation at that step.

## Try it

The real component from `apps/editor`, rendered alone. The story loads the editor's `tokens.css` and `type.css` (but not `base.css`, so the editor's focus ring is not used here) and prints what the component reports. The first bar is the closed look; the second is open at the selected step.

## In detail

### Public surface

| Prop | Type | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tabs` | `{ id: string; label: string }[]` | required | Equal-width tabs, in order. |
| `active` | `string \| null` | required | `null` gives the closed look. |
| `onSelect` | `(id: string) => void` | required | Click, Enter or Space. |
| `label` | `string` | required | `aria-label` of the tablist. |
| `panelId` | `string` | — | `aria-controls` of the active tab. |

### Inputs → outputs

- `role="tablist"` of `role="tab"` buttons with `aria-selected`. The single tab stop is the
  focused tab, else the active one, else the first.
- Left, Right, Home and End move focus only (`rovingIndex`). Up and Down are left to the page.
- Closed: black tabs, `.t-xs` in `--color-on-bar`. Open: every tab gets a right and bottom
  `--rule`, and the active one is paper with ink text and no bottom rule.

### Invariants

- **Manual activation.** Selecting a step opens the operation's draft, which is a real action, so
  moving focus never selects.

### Callers / callees

No callers in the editor yet (the operation header in Block E). Callees: `rovingIndex` from
`lib/ui/keys.ts`.

### Tests

None for the component. `lib/ui/keys.test.ts` covers the arithmetic.

### Gotchas & rejected alternatives

- **Height follows the container** (`height: 100%`, at least `--bar-h-group`). In the closed
  header, the tabs fill what the console strip leaves of `--op-header-h`.
