## Overview

`Collapsible` is the black header bar that opens and closes a part of the sidebar. In the
section size, it heads Grid & Size, Tiles and Operations. In the smaller group size, it heads each
tile group. The title is on the left. On the right is a chevron that points up when open and down
when closed, and optionally some decoration, such as a closed tile group's thumbnails. A tile
group's bar can also rename the group (click the title) and holds a trash button before the
title, as the operation header does.

Whether a bar is open lasts only for the session. It is never saved in the tileset file.

## In detail

### Purpose

The shared disclosure for the redesign. `Section.svelte` (Block C) and
[`TileGroup`](/editor/tile-group/) (Block D) are built on it. The bar is `box-sizing: border-box`, so `min-height` is the Figma bar
height with its 4px padding inside. It renders the body bare, because the section body and the tile group
body have different padding and frames in Figma.

### Public surface

| Prop | Type | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | `string` | required | Uppercased by the text style. |
| `open` | `boolean` (bindable) | `true` | Shown or hidden. |
| `variant` | `"section" \| "group"` | `"section"` | `section`: `.t-section`, `--bar-h-section`. `group`: `.t-label`, `--bar-h-group`, 1px border. |
| `level` | `2 \| 3 \| 4` | 2 for section, 3 for group | Heading level of the bar. |
| `onRename` | `(title: string) => void` | — | Makes the title an [`InlineEdit`](/editor/ui-inline-edit/). |
| `note` | `string` | — | Text after the title, not edited with it (a duplicate name's `(t3)`). |
| `hint` | `string` | — | Explanation shown on hovering the title ([`Hint`](/editor/ui-hint/)). Only with `onRename`. |
| `actions` | `Snippet` | — | Controls before the title, e.g. the trash `IconButton`. |
| `extras` | `Snippet` | — | Decorative content beside the chevron. |
| `children` | `Snippet` | required | The body. |

### Inputs → outputs

- `<h{level}>` wrapping the bar. In the bar: `actions`, then the title (an `InlineEdit` when
  `onRename` is set), then a `<button aria-expanded aria-controls>` that fills the rest of the
  bar. Without `actions` or `onRename`, the title is inside that button and the whole bar
  toggles. With `onRename`, the button is named `Expand {title}` or `Collapse {title}`.
- With `onRename`, the `InlineEdit` and the `note` sit in a `Hint` wrapper, whose id goes to the
  `InlineEdit`'s `aria-describedby`. Without it, the note follows the title inside the button.
- The body is `<div id>` (from `$props.id()`) and is rendered only while open.
- The chevron rotates 180° when open, over `--dur-fast`.

### Invariants

- **Open state is never persisted.** `09` §12 postpones persisted editor state and **E4** keeps
  it out of `meta`, the same rule `Section.svelte` documents.
- **`extras` must not be interactive.** It sits inside the button.

### Callers / callees

Callers: `lib/Section.svelte`, `lib/sections/BreakpointsSection.svelte` (rules, `variant="group"`), `lib/tiles/TileGroup.svelte`. Callees: `lib/ui/Icon.svelte` (`fold`), `lib/ui/InlineEdit.svelte`, `lib/ui/Hint.svelte`.

### Tests

None (the vitest config cannot load `.svelte`).

### Gotchas & rejected alternatives

- **Heading around button**, not the reverse, so the sidebar keeps an outline and the button
  keeps the disclosure semantics. Same shape as `Section.svelte`.
- **Rename and delete sit outside the disclosure button.** Interactive content cannot be nested
  in a button, so the bar is split: actions and the editable title, then the toggle.
